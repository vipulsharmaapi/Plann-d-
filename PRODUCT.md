# Plann’d

Plann’d helps people in Jaipur find small social plans, request to join, or host a plan at a local venue. This is the Supabase-connected application. Discovery, accounts, requests, approvals, chat, profiles and saved plans use the existing backend.

## People and purpose

The audience is Jaipur residents looking for a low-pressure coffee, sport, meal or shared activity with a few people nearby. A visitor needs to understand the activity, time, meeting place, host and remaining places before deciding to join. Joining an existing plan remains the primary journey; Join plans and Explore venues are visible peer modes, with posting available from either context.

## Product behavior

- Explore opens on upcoming Jaipur plans. Search, category, activity, date, neighbourhood, time, distance, open-place and women-only filters narrow the same live results. Dates use India time. A filtered-out plan must not remain selected in the adjacent detail view.
- Join plans and Explore venues sit directly below the compact Jaipur panorama. Each mode preserves its query and layout state when switching. Desktop plan discovery retains its list/detail or Google map composition; phone plans retain the floating List/Map switch, bottom navigation and full-screen plan detail sheet. Venue discovery uses list/detail columns on desktop and a dedicated detail view below 900px; Back restores the venue query and list scroll. Venue list and map share the same provider results and selection, separately from the plans map; Search this area is explicit.
- Request to join requires authentication. The review sheet allows an optional introduction of up to 500 characters. Sending creates a pending request; it does not reserve a place or grant chat access. The host accepts or declines requests. Existing capacity, eligibility, blocking and plan-status checks remain authoritative.
- Join introductions belong to the request and are private to the requester and host under the existing participant/host access policies. They are not public profile text or public plan content.
- Hosts and accepted members can use the existing group chat and permitted contact actions. Members can leave; requesters can withdraw pending requests; hosts can edit or cancel plans and manage members.
- Saved plans are private bookmarks, persisted per signed-in account in `saved_plans` and available in My plans → Saved. Saving does not request membership, reserve a place or notify the host. The saved state acknowledges a successful database response; failures remain visible with a retry path.
- Posting, signup/sign-in, email verification, profile editing, host approvals, inbox updates, safety/report/block controls, sharing, calendar export and in-app reminders remain connected to the existing services. Calendar downloads are snapshots and must be downloaded again after a plan changes. Optional reminders appear in the app 30 minutes before a plan; no new email, SMS or push reminder channel is implied.
- Posting retains the established field order: title, activity/open spots, venue, neighbourhood/date, start/end times and description. Google venue selection retains the exact coordinates and place ID; manually choosing a different meeting pin clears the place association. A visible time preview uses IST. Optional cost, skill level, booking, meeting instructions, menu, private group link and women-only settings remain available under the initially closed More details disclosure. Editing, reposting and atomic `save_plan` behavior remain connected to the existing backend. Explore Jaipur venues replaces the contents of the same Sheet with a picker while the post draft stays mounted. Selection remains tentative until Use this venue; Cancel, close, Back to your plan and browser Back preserve all fields and the previous confirmed venue. Make a plan here carries the selected provider venue through sign-in into the draft without publishing automatically. Manual venue-name edits clear stale provider associations. Failed writes preserve the draft; success appears only after the server confirms it.
- Venue search and discovery are public and retain the existing Google Places integration. Actual venue photos, ratings and attribution come from Google. The venue result is context for making a plan, not a booking, guarantee of availability or endorsement. Missing provider facts stay absent; missing photos use the building-icon fallback. Enter and the labelled Search venues button submit the same search.
- My plans and Inbox distinguish unresolved session, signed-out gate, signed-in loading, empty, failed and populated states. Guest content groups its explanation, Sign in and Keep exploring in one centered column. The existing Email code and Password methods preserve the initiating account destination or post intent. Signed-in fetch failures show recovery rather than a guest prompt.
- Header, profile, hosts, plan rows, members and messages share the Avatar component and canonical profile data. Persisted uploaded photos take precedence, then a saved fallback choice, current-name initials and the generic user icon. Failed image loads show a fallback. Upload and removal failures retain the last persisted image; successful changes update the shared profile cache. Guest navigation says Sign in.

## Truth and data boundaries

Production displays backend plans and truthful loading, empty and failure states. Do not add fictional plans, people, availability or success messages to fill a production screen. Local visual QA used fixture plans because discovery had no upcoming live plans during review; those fixtures are not production data or part of the source download.

The approved `jaipur-panorama.webp` is the compact header artwork, optimized from `../plannd-redesign/live-improvements/assets/jaipur-panorama-final.png`. It is a decorative generated illustration of Jaipur, with empty alt text; it does not document a venue. The earlier `jaipur-canopy.webp`, `jaipur-cafe.webp`, `jaipur-badminton.webp` and `jaipur-games.webp` remain available as illustrative activity artwork in `public/images/`. Details identify activity images as illustrative; Google venue photographs remain separate and attributed. `public/images/redesign-provenance.json` records all five shipped files, source paths and SHA-256 hashes. Real persisted profile photographs or the shared fallback chain represent users; sample design portraits must not represent live hosts.

The plan map renders the host’s stored latitude and longitude on Google Maps through the existing project key. Its label says “Meeting places from hosts”; it does not present fictional neighbourhood pins. Venue pin selection also uses Google Maps, and Google attribution remains visible with its content. If Google cannot load, hide its failed canvas and show recovery with retry; discovery remains available through List. A stored location is host-provided information, not independent venue verification.

The transactional migration `supabase/migrations/202611050001_saved_plans_and_introductions.sql` adds owner-scoped bookmark policies and the 500-character introduction constraint. Its application and database checks are recorded in `docs/OPERATIONS.md`. Existing request-access policies and the host-only response RPC remain in force. Historical bootstrap SQL contains demo seeds and must not be replayed to populate production.
